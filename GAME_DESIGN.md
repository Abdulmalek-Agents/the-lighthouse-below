# The Lighthouse Below
### A Cooperative Deep-Sea Investigation Horror Game

---

## 1. Vision Statement

> *"Four divers. One impossible city six kilometers down. The deeper you piece the truth together, the less you want to know."*

**The Lighthouse Below** is a 1–4 player cooperative atmospheric horror game in which a salvage crew explores the impossible ruins of a pre-human civilization discovered beneath the Mariana abyssal plain. Unlike the silly chaos of *Lethal Company*, this game treats horror with the gravitas of *Iron Lung* and the investigative depth of *Return of the Obra Dinn* — every dive is a mystery, every artifact is evidence, and every death is logged in a shared global archive.

Picture *Subnautica*'s awe, *Barotrauma*'s tension, and *Outer Wilds*' epiphany — fused into a session-based co-op horror loop.

## 3. Core Mechanics

### 3.1 The Dive Loop (15–25 min per session)

1. **Briefing** – Crew receives a salvage contract from the Tethys Foundation. Surface lighthouse rig is the hub.
2. **Descent** – Submersible (the *Anglerfish*) descends. Pressure, hull integrity, oxygen tick down.
3. **Exploration** – Crew exits via airlock into procedurally-assembled ruin chambers using diving suits.
4. **Discovery** – Players photograph anomalies, collect glyph fragments, recover artifacts.
5. **The Turn** – At random depth/triggers, *something* changes. The ruins shift. Entities awaken.
6. **Ascent** – Survivors must reach the *Anglerfish* before catastrophic events. Lost crew = lost discoveries unless body is recovered.
7. **The Archive** – Back at the lighthouse, players log findings into a *shared global codex* — discoveries persist across players worldwide.

### 3.2 Pillar Mechanics

#### A) The Glyph Codex (Investigation Core)
- Ancient glyphs are scattered as fragments throughout ruins.
- Players photograph them with their dive cameras.
- Glyphs partially auto-translate via a *cipher web* — each new glyph helps decode others (similar to Tunic / Chants of Sennaar).
- **Server-wide collaborative translation**: when 1% of global players photograph the same glyph, its meaning is "unlocked" for everyone — creating ARG-like cultural moments.

#### B) Pressure & Pact (Co-op Tension Core)
- Each suit has a finite **oxygen + pressure tolerance**.
- Pressure damage is *invisible*: you don't know your suit is compromised until you start hallucinating.
- **The Pact**: at session start, crew secretly chooses one of four contracts (Salvage / Survey / Sabotage / Sanctuary). The *Sabotage* role is a hidden traitor with their own win condition — but optional (toggleable per lobby). Adds Among Us layer without breaking immersion.

#### C) The Turning (Horror Core)
- Each ruin has a hidden "Turning Threshold" (collect too much / linger too long / decode forbidden glyph).
- When triggered, the ruin *physically rearranges*. Doors close. Geometry warps. The Anglerfish drifts away.
- New entities spawn — but always *witnessed*, never gore-spam. Inspired by *SOMA* design philosophy.

#### D) The Recorder (Diegetic Communication)
- Voice chat is range-based and degrades with depth.
- Players carry a *tape recorder* — short audio messages can be left in physical locations.
- Recordings persist *across runs and across players* — find a stranger's last words echoing through the ruins.

### 3.3 The Persistent Archive
- The Tethys Lighthouse hub displays *every* glyph, creature sketch, audio log, and ruin map ever collected on that player's account.
- Cross-references unlock cinematic "Revelations" — the macro-narrative is jigsaw-pieced together across hundreds of hours.
- **The Great Mystery**: Who built the city? Why is the surface forgetting it exists? This unfolds across seasonal content.

## 4. Unique Selling Points

1. **First serious-tone co-op horror with investigation as its core loop** — not combat, not stealth: *understanding*.
2. **Global cooperative codex** — your discoveries genuinely advance humanity's translation. ARG-tier engagement.
3. **Persistent diegetic memory** — tape recorders left in ruins are heard by future players. The world remembers.
4. **Optional hidden-traitor role** — competitive layer that doesn't break solo or trusted-group play.
5. **Replayable horror** — procedural ruin generation with hand-authored set-piece "anomalies" prevents fatigue.

## 9. Production Pillars (Dev Constraints)

| Pillar | Rule |
|---|---|
| **Atmosphere > Spectacle** | If a moment isn't earned by silence, it isn't earned |
| **Witnessed, not splattered** | Body horror is implied. The player imagines worse than we render |
| **Diegetic everything** | No floating HUDs. Wrist-mounted suit interface only |
| **The world remembers** | Every persistent system reinforces "you matter at scale" |
| **No combat as power fantasy** | Players can defend but never dominate |

## 10. Risk Analysis & Mitigations

| Risk | Likelihood | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| Genre saturation by 2027 | Medium | Investigation core is genuinely differentiated |
| Solo players feel locked out | High | Robust AI crew + tuned solo difficulty |
| Translation puzzle gating frustrates casuals | Medium | "Salvage mode" toggle skips deepest glyph layers |
| Horror fatigue cycles | Low | Persistent archive turns horror → wonder over time |
| Server costs for global codex | Medium | Codex is async — no realtime traffic, S3-tier cheap |
