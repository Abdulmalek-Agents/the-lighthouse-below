## Full Game Description

**The Lighthouse Below** is a single-player or 1–4 player cooperative horror game set six kilometers beneath the Mariana Trench. You and your crew are contracted by the **Tethys Foundation** — a shadowy salvage company — to investigate the impossible ruins of a pre-human civilization discovered at the bottom of the ocean.

Unlike the silly, chaotic tone of *Lethal Company*, this game treats horror with gravitas. The player's tools are not weapons — they are a **camera**, a **flashlight**, and a **tape recorder**. Every dive is a mystery: *Who built this city six kilometers deep? Why does the surface forget it exists?*

### Core Loop (per dive, ~15–25 minutes)

1. **Briefing** — Receive a salvage contract at your surface lighthouse rig
2. **Descent** — Pilot the submersible *Anglerfish-IV* down the abyssal trench
3. **Exploration** — Exit the airlock, swim through procedurally-generated ruin chambers
4. **Investigation** — Photograph alien glyphs, recover artifacts, decode an unknown language
5. **The Turning** — At a hidden threshold, the ruins begin to shift. Geometry warps. Entities awaken.
6. **Ascent** — Return to the *Anglerfish* before oxygen depletes
7. **The Archive** — Log discoveries into a persistent codex shared by all players globally

### The Breakthrough Mechanic

Glyphs you photograph contribute to a **server-wide collaborative translation effort**. When 1% of global players photograph the same glyph, its meaning is unlocked for *everyone* — creating ARG-tier cultural moments. You can also leave **tape-recorded messages** that future players will find in their own dives, weeks later. **The world remembers.**
